# Emberwood art pass 01

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, using `docs/mockups/01-visual-directions.png` as a style reference (left panel only). No image processing was applied. Assets are opaque standalone textures; gameplay text and stats are drawn live in Godot.

- `emberwood-table.png`: candlelit timber backdrop, independent of cards and lane frames.
- `fox.png`: engraved fox on aged parchment with space for live name and stats.
- `wolf.png`: matching engraved wolf on aged parchment.

Noto Serif is bundled with its SIL Open Font License under `assets/fonts/`. The hand now supports selection, sacrifice costs, and placement in the ritual sandbox.
